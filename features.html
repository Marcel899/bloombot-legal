Commands

Getting started

  • /helpSee what Bloombot can do, by topic.
  • /linkConnect your Roblox account and private server link.
  • /homeYour hub: trade ads, team finder, strategies and settings.
  • /pingCheck the bot is online.

Trading

  • /trade adPost a global trade ad.
  • /trade bannerMake a trading card picture, then post it as an ad.
  • /trade bumpMove your ad back to the bottom (every 5 minutes).
  • /wishlistUnits you want. Get a DM when an ad offers one.
  • Apps → Check tradeRight-click a trade ad to check if it's fair.

Values

  • /valueA unit's value, demand, price chart, stats and advanced value.
  • /value-hubPrice moves, what to hold, 30-day guesses and cheap units.
  • /value-calculatorWhat units are worth, or whether a trade is fair.

Stats

  • /searchUnit or enemy stats. Partial names work.
  • /dpsThis week's DPS ranking of every unit.

Levels

  • /levelYour level, XP and rank perks.
  • /leaderboardThe top Bloombot levels.
  • /ranksEvery rank and what it unlocks.

Server admins

  • /setupFirst-time setup of Bloombot's channels. Run this first.
  • /configChange channels, turn features on or off, set the value-update ping role.
  • /giveaway createCreate a giveaway with optional entry rules.
  • Apps → Report userRight-click a user to report them to the Bloombot team.
