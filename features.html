Getting started

  • See what Bloombot can do, by topic.
  • Connect your Roblox account and private server link.
  • Your hub: trade ads, team finder, strategies and settings.
  • Check the bot is online.

Trading

  • Post a global trade ad.
  • Make a trading card picture, then post it as an ad.
  • Move your ad back to the bottom (every 5 minutes).
  • Units you want. Get a DM when an ad offers one.
  • Right-click a trade ad to check if it's fair.

Values

  • A unit's value, demand, price chart, stats and advanced value.
  • Price moves, what to hold, 30-day guesses and cheap units.
  • What units are worth, or whether a trade is fair.

Stats

  • Unit or enemy stats. Partial names work.

Levels

Server admins

  • Create a giveaway with optional entry rules.
