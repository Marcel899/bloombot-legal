How it works

Every crate is a fresh roll

Opening crates doesn't make the next one luckier. At 1% you don't get the unit after 100 crates for sure: your chance is 1 - 0.99100 = 63%. That's why we never just multiply the chance by the crates.

Average is not guaranteed

The average is what it takes across many players. About 63% get the unit by then, and 37% don't. Without pity there is no number of crates that guarantees it.

Luck

We multiply the drop chance by your luck, up to 100%. 2x luck halves the average crates. Check in game which units your luck boost works on.

Pity

With pity, the crate at the pity count always drops that rarity. If the rarity has more than one unit, it can still be a different one. Pity counts are per rarity and reset when you pull one.

Drop chances and prices are from the in-game shop and can change with updates. Got different numbers? Just type them in. More crates on the Garden Tower Defense wiki.
