Advent calendar: 24 doors, 12 hidden units and an Infectia draw

27 November 2026·1 min read·Bloombot team

In this postOne door a dayWho can win a unitAlso new: News

The Bloombot advent calendar opens on 1 December. Here's how it works.

One door a day#

  • XP every day: each door gives 50 XP, and door 24 gives 250 XP. Doors open at 00:00 UTC and only today's door can be opened, so a missed day stays missed.
  • 12 hidden units: 3× Obsidian Rose, Surge Root, Hydrobloom Cannon and Splitjaw Flytrap are hidden on random days, each dropping at a secret time. The first trader to open (or check) the door after the drop wins it. A unit nobody finds carries over to the next days.
  • Infectia draw: every door you open is one ticket. The winner is drawn on 25 December.
  • Banners: open 12 doors for the Advent profile banner, all 24 for Golden Advent.

Who can win a unit#

You need a verified Roblox account, level 3 or higher and a Bloombot account at least 7 days old. Each trader can win one unit. Winners get a DM from Bloombot and the unit is traded to their verified Roblox account.

Also new: News#

You're reading it! The new News page has Bloombot news and GTD news, with every official GTD update since August and its codes. The bell in the top bar shows a dot when there's something new.
