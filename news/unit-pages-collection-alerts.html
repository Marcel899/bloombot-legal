New: unit pages, your collection and price alerts

27 November 2026·1 min read·Bloombot team

In this postTrack your unitsTrade fasterCommunity

Lots of new tools this week to help you trade smarter.

Track your units#

  • A page for every unit: open any unit on the value list for its own page with a clear 30-day trend, live trade ads and a link you can share.
  • My collection: add the units you own and see what they're worth together. Tick units to put them straight into a trade ad.
  • Price alerts: pick a unit and a price. Bloombot sends you a Discord DM or an email when the value goes above or below it.
  • Wishlist everywhere: your wishlist now lives on your account, so it's the same in Discord and on the site, and trade ads with your units are highlighted.

Trade faster#

  • Make an offer: send a real trade offer in messages, with a fair-trade check before you send it.
  • Live updates: new trade ads and messages show up instantly, and you get a "New message" toast on any page.

Community#

  • Strategy upvotes: upvote the strategies that work, sort by "Most upvoted" and see which map each one is for.
  • Weekly quests: new quests on the Levels page every week for extra XP.

Something not working? Tell us in the support server.
