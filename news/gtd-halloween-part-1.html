Halloween Part 1: Maze Massacre, Night Mode and the Skull Crate

24 October 2026·1 min read·GTD update notes

In this postHalloweenOther changesNew unitsCodes

A new Halloween map with 11 enemies and the Boneweaver boss, intense Night Mode, Blood Moon waves, Candy and a Skull Crate with 8 units.

Halloween#

  • Maze Massacre: new map with 7 levels. Clear the corn with your scythe!
  • 11 Halloween enemies, including the Boneweaver boss.
  • Night Mode: an intense new mode on every map and level that gives Candy.
  • Blood Moon waves: clear them for bonus Candy.
  • Candy: a new currency from Maze Massacre and Night Mode.
  • Skull Crate with 8 new units, 666 Candy.

Other changes#

  • Party Leader controls speed, skip and auto skip in every mode except PvP.
  • Slowing down in PvP no longer needs a majority.
  • New x2 Candy game pass, also giftable through trades.

New units#

  • Soulshard Stem (7,449 stock) and Ghost Lily (6,249 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

Codes#

  • GHOST: 500 Candy
  • CANDY: 750 Candy

Redeem them in the Codes menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Values often move after an update. Check the value list before you trade.
