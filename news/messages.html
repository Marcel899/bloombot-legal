Message other traders on the website

13 November 2026·1 min read·Bloombot team

You can now chat with other traders without leaving the site.

Messages#

  • Private chats with pictures and emojis. Open a chat from any trade ad or profile.
  • Safer trading: a chat filter, scam warnings and a report button. Reports keep the evidence so the team can review them.
  • Never miss a reply: an unread badge on your avatar, and email alerts if you turn them on in settings.

Farm calculator#

The Farm calculator works out how many Seeds and Fertilizer you really earn per hour, and how long you need to play to reach your goal.
