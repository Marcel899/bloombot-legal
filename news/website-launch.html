The Bloombot website is here!

3 November 2026·1 min read·Bloombot team

Everything Bloombot does in Discord, now in your browser too. Log in with Discord in one click.

What's on the site#

  • Values: every unit and gamepass with pictures, demand, status and 30-day trends. Open any unit for an interactive price chart, and star units to put them on your watchlist.
  • Trade calculator: add both sides and see Win, Fair or Lose right away, with the same 5% rule as /value-calculator. It suggests units to even the trade, and you can copy it for Discord, share a link or post it as a trade ad.
  • Trade ads: every live trade ad from every server, with the trader's Discord and Roblox. Post your own: pick units, set amounts or bulk, add offers or overpay, and it goes to every Bloombot server.
  • Strategies: browse community strategies or share your own. They show up in /home in Discord too.
  • Features & commands: every command, searchable.

Quick search#

Press Ctrl + K anywhere to search units, strategies, commands and pages.

Posting needs a linked account (/link). Values are estimates, not guarantees.
