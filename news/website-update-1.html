Website update: levels, crate calculator, profiles and more

7 November 2026·2 min read·Bloombot team

A lot has changed since launch. Here's what's new.

Levels

The new Levels page shows your animated level bar, the full rank road and every perk. Do one-time XP tasks (verify Roblox, join the server, post a trade ad, share a strategy, add the bot…) and claim the XP right on the site. Higher ranks unlock animated profile banners, a rank glow and shorter strategy cooldowns.

Crate calculator

All 21 crates in an in-game style summon window on the Crate calculator. Pick a unit and see your real chance, plus how much it costs if you're lucky, normal or unlucky. Luck boosts and pity are included.

Profiles and logins

  • Profiles: click any name to open their profile in a popup. Pick a profile banner, write an About me and show off your level. Verified accounts get a badge.
  • Google login and Roblox verify: log in with Discord or Google, and verify your Roblox account with a code in your Roblox About. Manage it all under Connected accounts in settings.

Better tools

  • Strategies: share in 4 easy steps (basics, units, how to play, share) with a live preview. Sort, filter by category or your own, and use Write/Preview with formatting buttons.
  • Trade ads: a redesigned composer with unit tiles and a live preview. Delete your own ads anytime.
  • Giveaways: a giveaway popup on every page with a live countdown. Do tasks for extra entries and watch your win chance.
  • Server dashboard: owners and admins can set up Bloombot from the browser: channels, roles and features, with search, permission warnings and change history.
  • Values: animated filters, rank badges, demand bars, coloured status tags and a new list view.

Everything else

  • A new home page with live trade ads, value moves and live server and user counts
  • A light mode toggle
  • A new top bar and a smoother phone menu
  • Better Ctrl + K search with filters and recent searches
  • Delete your own strategies or your whole account
  • Faster page loads and lots of phone fixes

Values are estimates, not guarantees. Found a bug? Tell us in the support server.
