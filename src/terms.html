2. What Bloombot does

Bloombot is a free, fan-made bot for the Roblox game Garden Tower Defense. It lets players post trade ads that are shared across every server running Bloombot, check unit values and trades, keep wishlists and value alerts, earn levels, and join giveaways. Features can change, be limited, or be removed at any time.

4. Trade ads

  • A trade ad is public. It is posted to the trade ad channels of every server in the Bloombot network and to the trade board, and stays up for 24 hours.
  • Ads must be about Garden Tower Defense items and be 7 to 200 characters long. External links are not allowed.
  • You can post one ad every 5 minutes. Do not repost the same ad to get around this.
  • Images are checked automatically for inappropriate content, and ads that break the rules are not posted.
  • Only offer items you actually have, and describe them honestly.

5. Trading and values

Bloombot only helps players find each other. Every trade happens inside Roblox, between players, at their own risk. We are not part of any trade and cannot undo, refund or replace anything.

Unit values, demand figures, trends and trade checks are estimates built from community value lists and trading activity. They can be wrong or out of date. They are not advice, and we are not responsible for decisions you make based on them.

6. Giveaways

  • Giveaways in a Discord server are run by that server, which is responsible for its own rules and for handing out prizes. Bloombot only provides the tool.
  • Giveaways on the Bloombot website are run by us. They are free: you never have to pay or buy anything to enter or to win, and paying doesn't improve your chances.
  • Each giveaway shows its prize, end time, entry tasks and any requirements (for example being in the Bloombot Discord server or a minimum level). Each completed task gives a set number of entries.
  • When a giveaway ends, the winner is drawn at random, weighted by entries, from entrants who still meet the requirements. The winner is shown on the website, and we contact them on Discord to hand over the prize.
  • Prizes are Garden Tower Defense items and are handed over by an in-game trade in Roblox. Prizes can't be swapped for money or anything outside the game. If the winner doesn't reply within 7 days, we can draw a new winner.
