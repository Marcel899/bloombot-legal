The short version

  • We only store what Bloombot needs to work: your Discord ID, linked Roblox account, trade ads, levels, wishlists and similar.
  • Bloombot only reads messages in Global Trade Ad Channels and messages that @mention it.
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads. The website has no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics, and its fonts come from this site, not from Google. If you choose Accept all, we only count your visit anonymously (see section 7).
  • Delete your account yourself on your website profile (Settings → Delete account), or ask in the support server and we delete your data within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU or UK, you have extra rights under the GDPR, listed in section 10.

This policy explains what information the Bloombot Discord bot and the Bloombot website ("Bloombot", "we", "us") collect when you use them, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

Bloombot is a free, non-commercial hobby project run by Marcel (Discord: marcel_899), a private developer who decides what data Bloombot uses and is the "controller" under data protection law. Bloombot has no company behind it, shows no ads and sells nothing. You can reach us in the Bloombot support server, or message marcel_899 on Discord. Privacy requests go straight to him.

2. Information we collect

Discord information. When you use a command, button or form, we receive your Discord user ID, username, and the server and channel IDs where you used it, as Discord sends them to every bot.

Your Bloombot account. If you register with /link or /home, or on the website, we store:

  • your Roblox username, and your Roblox user ID and avatar picture, looked up from Roblox's public API;
  • if you verify your Roblox account on the website: the one-time code we gave you, and whether it was found in the public "About" text of your Roblox profile. We read that text only when you press Check and don't keep it;
  • the private server link you give us;
  • your profile description, profile banner and your choice of avatar (Roblox or Discord);
  • the date you registered.

Content you post through Bloombot.

  • Trade ads: the text and image of your ad, when it was posted and when it expires (ads expire after 24 hours).
  • Trade ad history: a record of which user posted an ad and when, used for daily limits and statistics.
  • Unit demand: which units you looked for or offered in a trade ad on a given day. This feeds the demand figures in the value stats.
  • Team finds and strategies: the text, title and category you submit.
  • Reports: who reported whom, and the reason given.
  • Wishlists and value alerts: the units you add, so we can DM you when a matching trade ad or value change appears.
  • Giveaways: which giveaways you entered and won, which entry tasks you completed and how many entries you got. For website giveaways that ask you to be in the Bloombot Discord server, the bot keeps a list of the Discord IDs of that server's members. Accounts caught cheating can be blocked from giveaways, with a short reason.
  • XP tasks: which one-time website tasks you claimed XP for. For the "link Google" and "verify Roblox" tasks we keep the ID of that Google or Roblox account, so the same account can't earn the XP twice.
  • Invite tracking: which invite you used to join a server and who created it, so invites can be counted. When you leave, you stop counting toward the inviter's total.

Message content. Bloombot reads the text of messages only in two cases:

  • messages posted in a channel a server has set up as a Global Trade Ad Channel, which Bloombot turns into trade ads (stored as described above);
  • messages that @mention Bloombot, so it can reply. These are not stored.

Bloombot does not read, log or store any other messages.

Activity and levels. We store your XP, level and how many commands you have used, plus a log of commands used (command name, user ID, whether it worked, how long it took, and when) for statistics and troubleshooting.

Moderation. If staff take action against an account or server (for example a bot ban), we store the action, the IDs involved, the moderator and the reason.

Website login. When you log in on the Bloombot website with Discord, Discord gives us your Discord ID, username, display name, avatar and email address. If you log in with Google, Google gives us your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. Google logins are connected to your Discord account, because Bloombot accounts live in Discord. We use this to log you in and show your name. The only emails we send are alerts when someone messages you on the website, and you can turn those off in Message settings. We only post a trade ad when you send one yourself, and it is stored and shown like any other trade ad.

Website messages. When you message another trader on the website, we store the message, any picture you attach, who sent it, who it was for and when, so you both can read it. Pictures are re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes location and camera data, and only the two of you can open them. Messages that break the chat rules are kept for staff to review. When someone reports a message, staff get a copy of it (and its picture, if any) with the reason and any details given, and that copy stays even if the sender unsends it. You can unsend your own messages, delete a chat for yourself, block people and turn off email alerts.

Server settings and dashboard. For servers that set up Bloombot we store channel and role IDs, webhook links Bloombot created, enabled features, and who completed the setup. When a server admin changes settings on the website dashboard, we log who changed what and when, so the server's admins can see it.

Technical data on the website. Like every website, our host (Vercel) and our login and database provider (Supabase) receive your IP address, browser type and the page or request you made, and keep short-lived server logs for security and to keep the service running. We don't use these logs to build profiles. Pictures such as Discord avatars, Roblox avatars and Google profile pictures load straight from Discord's, Roblox's or Google's servers, which then see your IP address. Google's "Sign in with Google" button is loaded from Google only when you open the login window.

Error and server logs. When an error happens, details of it (including the command, user ID and server name and ID) are sent to a private developer channel. We also log when Bloombot joins or leaves a server.

3. How we use it, and why we're allowed to

  • To run the features you use: posting trade ads to other servers, profiles, trade checks, values, levels, wishlists, alerts, giveaways, website login and the server dashboard. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep Bloombot safe: checking images and text for inappropriate content, preventing spam, scams, alt accounts and abuse, enforcing the Terms, and keeping moderation records. Legal basis: our legitimate interest, and yours, in a safe trading community (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To show public statistics such as unit demand and leaderboards, and to fix bugs and improve Bloombot with command and error logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working, useful bot (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Optional browser storage and anonymous visit counts (see section 7). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw any time in Cookie settings.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described below. Automatic image and text checks can stop a single trade ad, strategy or profile change from being posted. They never decide anything else about you, and you can always ask staff to look at a decision.

4. What other people can see

Trade ads, team finds, strategies, your profile (Roblox username, private server link, description, banner, avatar), levels and leaderboard positions are meant to be public. Trade ads are posted in every server that has opted into the global trade ad network. Live trade ads (the text, image, your Discord name and avatar, and your linked Roblox account) are also shown on the Bloombot website to anyone logged in. Strategies are shown publicly on the website with your Discord name and avatar. Giveaway winners may be announced with their Discord name.

5. Services we rely on

  • Discord, which delivers all messages and interactions, and holds our private log and backup channels (Discord Privacy Policy).
  • Google, if you choose to log in with Google (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Roblox public API, to look up Roblox user IDs and avatars from a username, and to read your public About text when you verify your Roblox account (Roblox Privacy Policy).
  • Sightengine (France), which checks images attached to trade ads and profiles for inappropriate content. The image is sent to Sightengine for this check (Sightengine Privacy Policy).
  • Wispbyte, the hosting provider where the bot and its database run.
  • Supabase, which handles the website's Discord and Google login and stores the website's data (live trade ads, profiles, giveaways, values) on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Vercel, which hosts this website (Vercel Privacy Policy).

These providers only handle data to run their part of Bloombot. Some of them (Discord, Google, Roblox, Vercel, and Supabase's parent company) are based in the USA, so your data may be processed there. Where a provider is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, that covers the transfer; otherwise the provider uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Bloombot also reads public game data (such as unit values and the Garden Tower Defense wiki). That involves no personal data.

6. Storage and security

Data is kept in a database on our host. A daily backup of the database is posted to a private Discord channel that only the bot team can see. Access is limited to the bot team. The website only connects over HTTPS, and its database uses access rules so that you can only change your own data.

7. Cookies and local storage

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics. It only saves small things in your browser (cookies and local storage) so the site works. The first time you visit, we ask you to choose:

  • Essential (always on): your login session, light or dark mode, your cookie choice, and things you save yourself, like your watchlist or a strategy draft. These are needed for features you ask for, so they don't need consent.
  • Optional (only with Accept all): your recent searches, whether you closed popups such as the giveaway box, and an anonymous visit count. For the count, the site adds 1 to a total for the page, the hour, the website you came from, your device type (phone, tablet or computer) and your country. It also remembers the date of your last visit in your browser, so a visit counts once per day. No ID, cookie or IP address is saved with the count. To stop the same visit being counted many times, our database briefly keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, which changes every day and is deleted within an hour.

You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page). Picking Essential only removes the optional data straight away. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything.

8. How long we keep it

  • Trade ads and team finds stop showing after 24 hours.
  • The website's copy of a trade ad is removed within a few minutes of the ad ending.
  • Unit demand records are deleted after 400 days.
  • Command logs and trade ad history keep your user ID for 12 months. After that the ID is removed and only anonymous totals remain.
  • Error reports are kept only as long as we need them to fix the problem.
  • Database backups are deleted after 60 days.
  • Account, profile, website login, level, wishlist and other data is kept while you use Bloombot, or until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Moderation records and giveaway blocks may be kept longer to keep banned users and cheaters from coming back.

9. Children

Bloombot is for Discord users only, and Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you are under 16 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), ask a parent or guardian before you use Bloombot or accept optional cookies. If you think a child under 13 is using Bloombot, tell us in the support server and we will delete their data.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your own trade ads and strategies, and edit your profile, wishlist and alerts, with Bloombot's commands or on the website.
  • You can delete your whole Bloombot account on the website: open your profile, then Settings → Delete account. This removes your account link, profile, live trade ads, strategies, wishlist, alerts and website login straight away. Your level and XP stay, and trade ad copies already posted in server channels stay up. Ask in the support server if you want those removed too.
  • Server admins can remove Bloombot at any time; its settings for that server can be deleted on request.

Under data protection law (such as the EU and UK GDPR) you also have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • get your data in a common, machine-readable format to take elsewhere (portability);
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for optional browser storage: Cookie settings), without affecting anything done before;
  • complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

To use these rights, ask in the support server. We may ask you to show that the Discord account is yours, and we answer within 30 days.
