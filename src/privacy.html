The short version

  • We only store what Bloombot needs to work: your Discord ID, linked Roblox account, trade ads, levels, wishlists and similar.
  • Bloombot only reads messages in Global Trade Ad Channels and messages that @mention it.
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads.
  • Ask in the support server and we delete your data within 30 days.

This policy explains what information the Bloombot Discord bot ("Bloombot", "we", "us") collects when you use it, why, and what you can do about it. By using Bloombot you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Discord information. When you use a command, button or form, we receive your Discord user ID, username, and the server and channel IDs where you used it, as Discord sends them to every bot.

Your Bloombot account. If you register with /link or /home, we store:

  • your Roblox username, and your Roblox user ID and avatar picture, looked up from Roblox's public API;
  • the private server link you give us;
  • your profile description and your choice of avatar (Roblox or Discord);
  • the date you registered.

Content you post through Bloombot.

  • Trade ads: the text and image of your ad, when it was posted and when it expires (ads expire after 24 hours).
  • Trade ad history: a record of which user posted an ad and when, used for daily limits and statistics.
  • Unit demand: which units you looked for or offered in a trade ad on a given day. This feeds the demand figures in the value stats and is deleted after 400 days.
  • Team finds and strategies: the text, title and category you submit.
  • Reports: who reported whom, and the reason given.
  • Wishlists and value alerts: the units you add, so we can DM you when a matching trade ad or value change appears.
  • Giveaways: which giveaways you entered and won.
  • Invite tracking: which invite you used to join a server and who created it, so invites can be counted. When you leave, you stop counting toward the inviter's total.

Message content. Bloombot reads the text of messages only in two cases:

  • messages posted in a channel a server has set up as a Global Trade Ad Channel, which Bloombot turns into trade ads (stored as described above);
  • messages that @mention Bloombot, so it can reply. These are not stored.

Bloombot does not read, log or store any other messages.

Activity and levels. We store your XP, level and how many commands you have used, plus a log of commands used (command name, user ID, whether it worked, how long it took, and when) for statistics and troubleshooting.

Moderation. If staff take action against an account or server (for example a bot ban), we store the action, the IDs involved, the moderator and the reason.

Server settings. For servers that set up Bloombot we store channel and role IDs, webhook links Bloombot created, enabled features, and who completed the setup.

Error and server logs. When an error happens, details of it (including the command, user ID and server name and ID) are sent to a private developer channel. We also log when Bloombot joins or leaves a server.

2. How we use it

  • to run Bloombot's features: posting trade ads to other servers, profiles, trade checks, values, levels, wishlists, alerts and giveaways;
  • to show public statistics such as unit demand and leaderboards;
  • to prevent spam and abuse and enforce these terms;
  • to fix bugs and improve the bot.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as described below.

3. What other people can see

Trade ads, team finds, strategies, your profile (Roblox username, private server link, description, avatar), levels and leaderboard positions are meant to be public. Trade ads are posted in every server that has opted into the global trade ad network.

4. Services we rely on

  • Discord, which delivers all messages and interactions (Discord Privacy Policy).
  • Roblox public API, to look up Roblox user IDs and avatars from a username.
  • Sightengine, which checks images attached to trade ads and profiles for inappropriate content. The image is sent to Sightengine for this check (Sightengine Privacy Policy).
  • Wispbyte, the hosting provider where the bot and its database run.

Bloombot also reads public game data (such as unit values and the Garden Tower Defense wiki). That involves no personal data.

5. Storage and security

Data is kept in a database on our host. A daily backup of the database is posted to a private Discord channel that only the bot team can see. Access is limited to the bot team.

6. How long we keep it

  • Trade ads and team finds stop showing after 24 hours.
  • Unit demand records are deleted after 400 days.
  • Account, level, wishlist and other data is kept while you use Bloombot, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Moderation records may be kept longer to keep banned users from returning.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your own trade ads and edit your profile, wishlist and alerts with Bloombot's commands.
  • To see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, ask in the support server. We answer within 30 days.
  • Server admins can remove Bloombot at any time; its settings for that server can be deleted on request.

8. Children

Bloombot is for Discord users only, and Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use Bloombot after a change means you accept it.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: join the Bloombot support server.
