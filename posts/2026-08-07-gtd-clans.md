Create or join a Clan, chat with your team, do Clan Quests, climb Clan Ranks and hang out in your own Clan Base.

## Clans

- **Clans UI**: create or find a Clan.
- **Clan Chat** to talk with your Clan.
- **Clan Quests**: finish quests together to level up your Clan.
- **Clan Ranks** unlock as your Clan levels up, and **Clan Roles** let you set who does what.
- **Clan Base**: teleport to your own base to hang out. Customisation is coming soon.
- The first 500 Clans to reach **Level 20** get **Rose Royale**!

## New units

- **Orbarrage Bloom** (11,349 stock) and **Sunfire Blossom** (8,249 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Codes

- `CLAN`: 500 Seeds
- `BASE`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

More Clan features are on the way, including Clan Wars.
