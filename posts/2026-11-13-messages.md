You can now chat with other traders without leaving the site.

## Messages

- :envelope: **Private chats** with pictures and emojis. Open a chat from any trade ad or profile.
- :shield: **Safer trading**: a chat filter, scam warnings and a report button. Reports keep the evidence so the team can review them.
- :bell: **Never miss a reply**: an unread badge on your avatar, and email alerts if you turn them on in settings.

## Farm calculator

:seeds: The **[Farm calculator](farm.html)** works out how many Seeds and Fertilizer you really earn per hour, and how long you need to play to reach your goal.
