Freeze now stacks to stall enemies, top units get locked in PvP each week and two new limited stock units arrive.

## New features

- **Freeze rework**: stack 10 Freeze hits to stall enemies for a moment.
- **PvP locked units**: some Godly and Exclusive units are locked in PvP Mode each week.
- More units join the Endless Mode ban pool every week.
- New **enemy attack type icons**: Slowness, Acid, Health Loss, Vulnerability, Hypnotize, Stun and Knockback.
- Mortarfern targeting revamp, and better phone and tablet support.

## New units

- **Parasite Plant** (11,111 stock): stacks Contagious and hypnotizes enemies.
- **Shockfrost Sprout** (9,999 stock): electricity and freeze.
- Both are limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Codes

- `SHOCKFROST`: 500 Seeds
- `PARASITE`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
