New Classic Mode reward units on four maps, Pity on all Classic Mode units, and big wave and Seeds overhauls for Tropical Takedown and Radioactive Rampage.

## New units

- **Spiritwood**: new Classic Mode reward in Enchanted Encounter.
- **Dual Stemstorm**: new Classic Mode reward in Garden Grind.
- **Bubble Blossom**: new Classic Mode reward in Aqua Ambush.
- **Fire Dragon**: new Classic Mode reward in Volcanic Vengeance.
- **Dual Bellflower** (10,499 stock) and **Beaconbud** (8,499 stock): new limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Changes

- All Classic Mode units are trialling **Pity**. The team wants your feedback on it.
- **Tropical Takedown** and **Radioactive Rampage**: waves and Seeds overhauled.
- **Endless Mode** changes, with more tweaks coming based on feedback.
- Invite friends: they get 1,000 Seeds free when they join for the first time.
- Console Global Trading UI issues, Treasure Trap bugs and leaderboard bugs fixed.

## Codes

- `CLASSICMODE`: 500 Seeds
- `PITY`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
