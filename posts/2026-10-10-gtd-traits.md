22 new Traits, the Fertilizer currency to roll them, free Trait Incubators, and a map wave overhaul to fight power creep.

## Traits

- **22 new Traits** like High Roller, Deadeye and Overbloom, in the **Traits Laboratory** (needs 15+ Victories).
- **Fertilizer**: a new currency you earn for free from Daily Rewards, normal play, VIP and more.
- Spend Fertilizer to **instantly roll** a unit's Trait, or remove Traits you don't want.
- **Trait Incubators** give free Traits, even offline. 15 minutes to 12 hours depending on rarity. Unlock up to 4.
- New game passes: **Auto Roll** (pick the Traits you want and a spending limit) and **x2 Fertilizer**. Both can be gifted through trades.

## Other changes

- **Map wave overhaul** for more balanced Seeds, to control unit and Trait power creep.
- Endless Mode wave scaling changes.
- Cycling Quests can be refreshed, with a shared Clan cooldown.
- New **Sneaks UI** with exclusive previews of upcoming updates.
- Skippable **cutscenes** on all Classic Mode maps.

## New units

- **Graviflora** (12,449 stock): a new attack type that pulls enemies together with gravity.
- **Signal Stem** (9,999 stock): red light, green light!

## Codes

- `TRAITS`: 25 Fertilizer
- `LAB`: 50 Fertilizer

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
