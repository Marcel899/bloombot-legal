A lot has changed since launch. Here's what's new.

## Levels

:medal_gold: The new **[Levels](levels.html)** page shows your animated level bar, the full rank road and every perk. Do one-time **XP tasks** (verify Roblox, join the server, post a trade ad, share a strategy, add the bot…) and claim the XP right on the site. Higher ranks unlock animated profile banners, a rank glow and shorter strategy cooldowns.

## Crate calculator

:crate: All 21 crates in an in-game style summon window on the **[Crate calculator](crates.html)**. Pick a unit and see your real chance, plus how much it costs if you're lucky, normal or unlucky. Luck boosts and pity are included.

## Profiles and logins

- :task_user: **Profiles**: click any name to open their profile in a popup. Pick a profile banner, write an About me and show off your level. Verified accounts get a badge.
- :google_stud: **Google login and Roblox verify**: log in with Discord or Google, and verify your Roblox account with a code in your Roblox About. Manage it all under **Connected accounts** in settings.

## Better tools

- :bullseye: **Strategies**: share in 4 easy steps (basics, units, how to play, share) with a live preview. Sort, filter by category or your own, and use Write/Preview with formatting buttons.
- :trading: **Trade ads**: a redesigned composer with unit tiles and a live preview. Delete your own ads anytime.
- :gift: **Giveaways**: a giveaway popup on every page with a live countdown. Do tasks for extra entries and watch your win chance.
- :gear: **[Server dashboard](dashboard.html)**: owners and admins can set up Bloombot from the browser: channels, roles and features, with search, permission warnings and change history.
- :dollar: **Values**: animated filters, rank badges, demand bars, coloured status tags and a new list view.

## Everything else

- A new home page with live trade ads, value moves and live server and user counts
- A light mode toggle
- A new top bar and a smoother phone menu
- Better Ctrl + K search with filters and recent searches
- Delete your own strategies or your whole account
- Faster page loads and lots of phone fixes
