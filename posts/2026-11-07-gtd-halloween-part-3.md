Team up in 2 vs 2 PvP, play Fantasy Frontier Survival, get free x1.5 and x2 Survival speed, and Daily Rewards that never end.

## New features

- **2 vs 2 PvP trial**: team up in Casual PvP. Ranked is coming soon.
- **Fantasy Frontier Island Survival Mode**, including Endless Survival leaderboards.
- **x1.5 and x2 speed in Survival** are now free for everyone.
- **Daily Rewards from Day 16 to infinity**: crates, Seeds, Fertilizer, x2 Luck, x2 Seeds, Godly units and more.
- **Clan tab** on leaderboards.
- Sandbox shares a placement limit: up to 80 units with 4 players.
- New Inventory sorting by amount owned and locked.

## New units

- **Silkshade** (8,449 stock) and **Skeleton Flytrap** (7,249 stock): limited stock units, available in Candy.

## Heads up

- On 9 November the team said the camera and player position reset after a game, which broke macros, was **not intended**. It came from a new map loading system and is **fixed in new servers**.
