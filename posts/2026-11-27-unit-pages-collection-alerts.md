Lots of new tools this week to help you trade smarter.

## Track your units

- :dollar: **A page for every unit**: open any unit on the [value list](values.html) for its own page with a clear 30-day trend, live trade ads and a link you can share.
- :gem: **[My collection](collection.html)**: add the units you own and see what they're worth together. Tick units to put them straight into a trade ad.
- :bell: **[Price alerts](alerts.html)**: pick a unit and a price. Bloombot sends you a Discord DM or an email when the value goes above or below it.
- :wish_star: **Wishlist everywhere**: your wishlist now lives on your account, so it's the same in Discord and on the site, and trade ads with your units are highlighted.

## Trade faster

- :offer: **Make an offer**: send a real trade offer in messages, with a fair-trade check before you send it.
- :lightning: **Live updates**: new trade ads and messages show up instantly, and you get a "New message" toast on any page.

## Community

- :bullseye: **Strategy upvotes**: upvote the strategies that work, sort by "Most upvoted" and see which map each one is for.
- :medal_gold: **Weekly quests**: new quests on the [Levels](levels.html) page every week for extra XP.
