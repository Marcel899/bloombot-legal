Clans can grow to 25 players and get custom icons, Clan Quests got easier, and PvP matchmaking is much faster.

## Clans

- Max Clan size goes from **20 to 25**, unlocked at Clan Level 41.
- **Custom Clan icons** at Clan Level 30.
- Clan Quests need less and give **more XP**.
- Your Clan now shows on your **Profile**, and Clans can be renamed every 7 days.

## PvP

- **Matchmaking overhaul** with much faster matchmaking.

## New units

- **Sprinklord** (10,449 stock) and **Electrosprout** (9,999 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Fixes

- Clan tags not showing above players, Clan Quest issues, Sundialstalk and 1st Bloomday Cake bugs fixed.

## Codes

- `SPRINKLORD`: 500 Seeds
- `ELECTROSPROUT`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
