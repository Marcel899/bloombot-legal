15 days of Daily Reward units, Autoplay, a big PvP Mode overhaul with a new season, and Pity on the Original Crate.

## New features

- **Autoplay**: turn it on and off with the Play and Stop buttons.
- **Daily Reward units**: 15 days of units, including a **mystery exclusive unit**. Made for new players, but everyone can claim them.
- **x1.5 Seeds** for your first Victory on every map and level.
- **Pity** on the Original Crate, going up with every summon.
- Story Chapter 1 is much easier, and Graveyard Gauntlet waves were overhauled.

## PvP Mode

- ELO compressed and rebalanced, with more matchmaking improvements.
- ELO gain or loss shown after PvP waves, plus a **Match Now** button.
- Warnings and punishments for leaving Ranked early.
- **Season refresh**: the top 50 get Gold, Silver or Bronze Venus Flytrap or Glass Dashroom. ELO was reduced for the new season.

## Endless Mode

- More units join the ban pool each week.
- Witchleaf, Transfiguration Flower and Viridian Branch are now always banned together.

## New units

- **Enchanterberries** (13,449 stock) and **Drainroot** (7,999 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Codes

- `DAILY`: 500 Seeds
- `AUTOPLAY`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
