Garden Tower Defense got its holiday update on Saturday, 21 November.

## What's new

- :news_turkey: **Turkey Mode**: a new holiday mode to play.

GTD updates every **Saturday at 13:00 UTC**. Values often move after an update, so check the [value list](values.html) before you trade, and use the [trade calculator](calculator.html) to see if a deal is fair.
