Turn every enemy into an Evil Turkey, open Global Trading straight from the Trade UI, and grab two new Thanksgiving units.

## Thanksgiving

- **Turkey Mode**: turn it on in the Pick Map UI to turn all enemies into **Evil Turkeys**!
- **Turkey Hut** (11,449 stock) and **Cornucopia** (8,249 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Trading

- **Quick access** to Global Trading from the Trade UI.
- Global Trading adverts now show on **profiles**.
- Players who come through Global Trading adverts are **highlighted**.

## Other changes

- AFK Zen Garden plots 5 and 6 now give **Seeds and Fertilizer**, and some units earn more Seeds with a higher cap.
- The Sandbox DPS dummy can **move**, to test Freeze, Knockback and more.
- Fewer boss notifications during waves, and a better Spectate UI.
- **Boost previews** while placing units, spawned unit stats, and a new **Enemy Stats UI**.

## Codes

- `TURKEY`: 500 Seeds
- `THANKSGIVING`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
