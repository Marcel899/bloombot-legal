Decorate your Clan Base, show it off to everyone, and summon base items with the new Clan Stars currency.

## Clan Base

- **Custom Clan Base**: decorate your base.
- **Public Clan Bases**: visit any Clan's base from the Clan UI.
- Clan owners and leaders can **kick** players from the base.
- **Clan Base Item Summon** with the new **Clan Stars** currency.

## New units

- **Medusa Flower** (10,449 stock) and **Bouncebloom** (7,999 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Fixes

- Sprinklord visuals, Timekeeper bugs and Survival Mode enemies pausing or stopping fixed.
- Login Streak issues were fixed later the same day.

## Codes

- `BOUNCE`: 500 Seeds
- `MEDUSA`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Saturday updates are back from 29 August.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
