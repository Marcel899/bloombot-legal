A Daily Reward exclusive unit, a Unit Keyword guide, shorter and faster PvP matches and easier Fantasy Frontier waves.

## New features

- **Trithorn**: new Daily Reward exclusive unit on Day 15.
- **Unit Keyword UI**: press '?' in the Inventory or Index to see attack types and how they link.
- **Weekly Content Creator rewards**.
- Console controls improved, and Chocolate, Knockback and Hypnotize show on Unit Stats.
- Fantasy Frontier waves rebalanced: **easier with more Seeds**.
- New unit collections in the checklist: Fantasy and Daily Rewards.

## PvP Mode

- Matches are **30 waves instead of 50**, with less waiting and earlier enemy summons.
- Losing with more than 6,475 ELO gives **7.5% of your ELO** to the winner.

## New units

- **Scopeleaf** (13,449 stock) and **Triangrove** (9,333 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Codes

- `TRIANGLE`: 500 Seeds
- `SNIPER`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
