A PvP season refresh, friends leaderboards, an option to hide in-game notifications and two new Candy units.

## New features

- **PvP season refresh**: the top 50 get Gold, Silver or Bronze Venus Flytrap or Glass Dashroom. ELO was reduced for the new season.
- PvP ELO changes for wins and losses, and better PvP waves.
- **Friends leaderboards**: compare your stats with friends.
- A setting to **turn off in-game notifications**.
- Better phone and tablet support.

## New units

- **Nullshade** (9,449 stock) and **Petriflora** (7,249 stock): limited stock units, available in **Candy**.

## Codes

- `PETRIFLORA`: 500 Candy
- `NULLSHADE`: 750 Candy

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
