Everything Bloombot does in Discord, now in your browser too. Log in with Discord in one click.

## What's on the site

- :dollar: **[Values](values.html)**: every unit and gamepass with pictures, demand, status and 30-day trends. Open any unit for an interactive price chart, and star units to put them on your watchlist.
- :calculator: **[Trade calculator](calculator.html)**: add both sides and see Win, Fair or Lose right away, with the same 5% rule as `/value-calculator`. It suggests units to even the trade, and you can copy it for Discord, share a link or post it as a trade ad.
- :trading: **[Trade ads](trades.html)**: every live trade ad from every server, with the trader's Discord and Roblox. Post your own: pick units, set amounts or bulk, add offers or overpay, and it goes to every Bloombot server.
- :bullseye: **[Strategies](strategies.html)**: browse community strategies or share your own. They show up in `/home` in Discord too.
- :gem: **[Features & commands](features.html)**: every command, searchable.

## Quick search

Press **Ctrl + K** anywhere to search units, strategies, commands and pages.

-# Posting needs a linked account (`/link`). Values are estimates, not guarantees.
