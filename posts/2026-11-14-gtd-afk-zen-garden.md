Plant units in your AFK Zen Garden to earn Seeds, livestream and watch games, and play Ranked 2 vs 2 PvP.

## AFK Zen Garden

- Plant your units in your garden to **earn Seeds**. Stay AFK for full Seeds or go offline for 10%.
- **Lucky Runestones** can spawn rare units.
- **6 plots**: 1 free, 1 VIP, the rest for Seeds. Plots 5 and 6 earn up to **200 Fertilizer a day**.
- New **Auto Collect Seeds** game pass, also giftable through trades.

## New features

- **Spectating**: livestream your games, watch others and join friends mid-game, with privacy settings.
- **Ranked 2 vs 2 PvP**, Season 1 is live. The PvP map is in Sandbox too.
- **Personal best trackers** for speed runs and Endless Mode.
- **Auto Upgrade All** button and ability active time in the Ability UI.
- New Settings UI and new unit and enemy sounds.

## New units

- **Oracle Bloom** (9,449 stock) and **Rageroot** (7,249 stock): limited stock units, available in Candy.

## Codes

- `ZEN`: 500 Candy
- `LIVESTREAM`: 750 Candy

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
