Lock your special units so they can't be deleted or traded by accident, plus two new limited stock units.

## New features

- **Unit Lock**: stops special units from being deleted, traded and so on. Toggle it in the Inventory.
- Players available for **global matchmaking** now show on every map and level when it's on.

## New units

- **Slay Stem** (14,449 stock) and **Ampiflora** (9,949 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds. A fix for Ampiflora followed soon after.

## Fixes

- Survival Mode enemies getting stuck and the Trade UI crashing fixed.

## Codes

- `SLAY`: 500 Seeds
- `POTION`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

A Daily Rewards exclusive unit is coming soon.

Values often move after an update. Check the [value list](values.html) before you trade.
