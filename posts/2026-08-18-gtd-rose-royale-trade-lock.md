No Rose Royale units were duplicated, and purchases on trade-locked accounts don't affect unit stock.

## Rose Royale

- After the Friday update some players saw what looked like duplicate Rose Royale summons. It was only a **visual bug** in chat and the stock 'exist' count. **No Rose Royale was duplicated.**

## Trade-locked accounts

- Accounts that broke the rules are **permanently trade locked**.
- Anything they buy is a Seed sink and is **not counted in unit stock**, so it doesn't change stock for anyone else.
