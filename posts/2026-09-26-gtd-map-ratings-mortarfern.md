Skulls now show how hard each level is, the Ability UI is smaller, storage holds up to 7,525 units and a new PvP season starts.

## New features

- **New map rating system**: Easy, Normal and Hard are gone. Skulls now show the difficulty of each level.
- **Ability UI overhaul**: more compact, collapsible, with unit highlights.
- New **unit storage tiers**: hold up to **7,525 units**.
- **PvP season refresh**: the top 50 get Gold, Silver or Bronze Venus Flytrap or Glass Dashroom.

## New units

- **Mortarfern** (12,749 stock): choose mortar targets with infinite range.
- **Chronorchid** (9,999 stock): teleports enemies and stops time.
- Both are limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Fixes

- Unit selling exploit, Ampiflora bugs, PvP Mode and leaderboard issues fixed.

## Codes

- `MORTAR`: 500 Seeds
- `TIMESTOP`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.
