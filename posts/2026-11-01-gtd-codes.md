Four new Garden Tower Defense codes came out at the start of November. Each one gives free Candy.

## The codes

- `PETRIFLORA`: 500 Candy
- `NULLSHADE`: 750 Candy
- `GHOST`: 500 Candy
- `CANDY`: 750 Candy

## How to redeem

Open Garden Tower Defense in Roblox, open the **Codes** menu, type a code exactly as written and press redeem.

-# Codes run out after a while. If one doesn't work any more, it has expired.
