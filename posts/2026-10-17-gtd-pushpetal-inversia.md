PvP gets x2 and x3 speed, Survival Mode comes to Sandbox and two new limited stock units drop.

## New features

- **x2 and x3 speed in PvP Mode** (both players have to agree).
- **Survival Mode** is now in Sandbox Mode.
- **Unequip all** button in the Inventory and Sandbox.
- Skippable cutscenes on all Survival Mode maps.

## New units

- **Pushpetal** (9,449 stock) and **Inversia** (6,499 stock): limited stock units, available in Seeds.

## Fixes

- Chicken Coop in Survival, Signal Stem bugs, and transformed enemies keeping their speed in later waves.

## Codes

- `PUSHPETAL`: 50 Fertilizer
- `INVERSIA`: 750 Seeds

Redeem them in the **Codes** menu in Garden Tower Defense. Codes stop working after a while.

The Halloween event starts next week.
