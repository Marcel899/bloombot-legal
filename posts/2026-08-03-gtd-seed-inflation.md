The GTD team is fighting Seed inflation caused by bot farms and power creep. Defeat Seeds are lower and some maps give different Seeds.

## What happened

- Average Seeds balances have gone up a lot since May 2026.
- **Bot farms** farmed Seeds from defeats, mostly on Volcanic Vengeance Hell, which only has a 0.53% win rate but is played more than every other map and level combined.
- Unit **power creep** plus bigger Seed rewards on later maps added to it.

## What the team changed

- Defeats now give a smaller share of the Victory Seeds.
- Seeds were adjusted on some maps, so units keep their trade value.
- Some maps and **all Survival maps** were made **easier**.

The team says these changes mainly hit bot farms and protect the economy for normal players.
