The Bloombot [advent calendar](advent.html) opens on **1 December**. Here's how it works.

## One door a day

- :lightning: **XP every day**: each door gives **50 XP**, and door 24 gives **250 XP**. Doors open at 00:00 UTC and only today's door can be opened, so a missed day stays missed.
- :gift: **12 hidden units**: 3× Obsidian Rose, Surge Root, Hydrobloom Cannon and Splitjaw Flytrap are hidden on random days, each dropping at a secret time. The first trader to open (or check) the door after the drop wins it. A unit nobody finds carries over to the next days.
- :wish_star: **Infectia draw**: every door you open is one ticket. The winner is drawn on **25 December**.
- :medal_gold: **Banners**: open 12 doors for the **Advent** profile banner, all 24 for **Golden Advent**.

## Who can win a unit

You need a verified Roblox account, level 3 or higher and a Bloombot account at least 7 days old. Each trader can win one unit. Winners get a DM from Bloombot and the unit is traded to their verified Roblox account.

## Also new: News

:envelope: You're reading it! The new [News](news.html) page has **Bloombot news** and **GTD news**, with every official GTD update since August and its codes. The bell in the top bar shows a dot when there's something new.
