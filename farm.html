How it works

Waiting counts too

A 12 minute run with 10 seconds of loading is really 12:10. We add the time between runs, so 3600 ÷ 730 = 4.9 runs an hour, not 5.

No rounding

Per hour is the real average, so 12:30 runs give 4.8 runs an hour instead of being rounded down to 4.

Only finished runs pay

For your play time and goals we count whole runs, because you get nothing for a run you stop halfway.
