Message Content intent

A server admin picks one channel as the Global Trade Ad Channel. Members post a trade ad there as a normal message, with text and one image. Bloombot reads only that channel. It checks the text (7 to 200 characters, no external links, a filter for disallowed words) and the image, then posts the ad to the trade ad channels of every server using Bloombot and to the trade board. Messages in any other channel, and messages from bots or webhooks, are ignored.

Members' messages in #global-trade-ads, turned into trade ads and posted to 25 to 26 servers. Each ad expires after 24 hours.

The channel explains to members that a message posted there becomes a trade ad.

When a message breaks the rules (here, posting again within 5 minutes), Bloombot DMs the member the reason and gives their text back. Other members get a DM when an ad offers an item on their wishlist.

Server Members intent

Bloombot uses this intent for invite tracking. When a member joins a server, it records which invite they used and who created it, so it can count how many people each member has invited (invites_required). When a member leaves, they no longer count toward the inviter's total. Staff tools (support lookups and reports) also look members up by ID or username and check their roles.

Invite tracking: the invites_required option counts the members someone has invited.
