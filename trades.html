About Bloombot trade ads

What are Bloombot trade ads?

Garden Tower Defense trade ads posted in any server running Bloombot. Each ad shows up in every other Bloombot server and on this page.

Why do I need to log in?

Trade ads are only shown to logged-in users, so traders can see who they are dealing with and reach them.

How do I post a trade ad?

Log in and press New trade ad, or use /trade ad in a server with Bloombot. Unit names turn into icons.

What do LF and NLF mean?

LF means looking for: the units someone wants. NLF means not looking for: units they will not take.
