How it works

Link your account

Run /link and add your Roblox username and private server link.

Post a trade ad

Write your ad in your server's Global Trade Ad Channel. Bloombot shares it with every server in the network.

Get offers

Traders DM you, and Bloombot DMs you when a unit on your wishlist is offered.
